RMU 2026 Instructions for Purchasing Materials

2025-10-21

I. Education Discount Application Process

* DJI sponsorship benefits application: Review the announcement " Announcement on DJI Sponsorship Benefits and Application Process ( https://www.robomaster.com/zh-CN/resource/pages/announcement/1889 ) ", complete the DJI sponsorship benefits application questionnaire linked in that announcement, and submit your benefits application and Taobao account registration.
* Eligible groups: Teams that have registered for and passed the registration review for RMU 2026  (including the RMUC and RMUL) or ROBOCON 2026.

For the ROBOCON-related education discount purchase procedure and the detailed list of items, see subsequent announcements.

II. Material Details

Special notes regarding the Referee System onboard modules:

1.The Referee System onboard modules are designed to serve the RMU competitions. The modules sold are the same as those used on the official website for the current season (see the competition rules and related documents for exact applicability). Future feature updates will be driven by the competition rules and may add or remove functions or change module support (some modules may become unusable after iterations). In addition, the serial protocol will be gradually reduced, with self-detectable data being removed.

2.The retail version of the Referee System onboard modules cannot be used in official competition. Teams are advised to prioritize using the onboard modules loaned/provided for the current season when preparing for competition to minimize procurement expenses.
