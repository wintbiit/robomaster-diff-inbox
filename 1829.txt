Notice on RMUL 2025 (North America) Final Robot Assessment

2025-04-01

*Eligible Participants:*

1.3V3 Match: All overseas participating teams that have passed the Season Planning phase must participate in this phase.

2..Infantry Match: All participating teams that have passed the Season Planning phase but have not undergone the full assessment for the 3V3 Match are exempt from additional submissions if they are concurrently participating in the 3V3 Match.

3.Engineer Challenge: All Engineer Challenge participating teams that have passed the Season Planning phase

*Submission Method:*

Submit as a team via the questionnaire filled by the team captain: [ CLICK HERE ( https://djistore.wjx.cn/vm/h7t02jY.aspx ) ].

*Submission Period:* April 15, 2025, 18:00 – April 17, 2025, 18:00 (Beijing Time)

*Submission Opportunity:* Only 1 submission attempt allowed.

*Passing Criteria:* Meeting certain progress requirements.

*Final Robot Assessment:* Record video materials following the order specified in RoboMaster 2025 University Series Technical Progress Assessment Criteria ( https://www.robomaster.com/en-US/resource/pages/announcement/1778 ) to showcase the actual progress of each robot type (e.g., Infantry, Hero, Sentry).

*Scoring Note:* Completing functions beyond those listed in the guidelines will earn extra points. Maximize your demonstration of progress.

*Anti-plagiarism measures:* To prevent reused video materials misrepresenting progress, teams must choose ONE of the following methods during filming:

a. [ CLICK HERE ( https://terra-1-g.djicdn.com/b2a076471c6c4b72b574a977334d3e05/Photo.png ) ] Download the armor diagram specialized for technical assessment, print and attach it to the robot’s armor plate or reserved installation position. Exception: Aerial robots without armor plates may adopt method b.

b. [ CLICK HERE ( https://terra-1-g.djicdn.com/b2a076471c6c4b72b574a977334d3e05/backdrop.png ) ] Download the background board specialized for technical assessment, display it clearly throughout the video (via A3 print, full-screen laptop, or physical board). No post-production synthesis allowed.

*Video Format:*

1.Video: The resolution must be higher than 720p; subtitles or commentaries should be included to explain each process in the video; only relevant content should be shown, with the video well-paced and its duration kept within 10 minutes; upload the video to Youtube and set a password

2.Filename: RMUL 2025 + School Name  + Final Robot Assessment

*Benefits for Passing Teams:*

1.Eligible to borrow a full set of  Referee System

2..Eligible to submit the Participation Feedback
