Notice on the RMUL 2025 Season Planning

2025-01-13

Participants: All registered RMUL teams from overseas.

How to Participate: Teams participate collectively. The Captain fill in the questionnaire https://djistore.wjx.cn/vm/QgnAWdV.aspx.

Submission Period: From 6:00 PM on January 21, 2025, to 6:00 PM on January 23, 2025 (Beijing Time).

Submission Opportunity: One chance.

Passing Criteria: Must achieve a "Pass" rating.

Assessment Content: Includes season planning and scheduling. Please carefully review the season planning requirements in Appendix 1 of the " RoboMaster 2025 University League Participant Manual ( https://www.robomaster.com/en-US/resource/pages/announcement/1774 )."

Please follow the season planning requirements in the appendix. Create a content template in the RoboMaster forum, select the appropriate template for writing, include plenty of charts and flow diagrams, ensure data is comprehensive, highlight key points, and maintain clear logic. Name the document in the following format: RMUL2025+University Name+Team Name+Section Name.

1.Season Plan: Refer to "[Template] RoboMaster University League Season Plan" (DOWNLOAD: Click to download ( https://terra-1-g.djicdn.com/b2a076471c6c4b72b574a977334d3e05/Template%20%20RoboMaster%202025%20University%20League%20Season%20Planning.docx ) ), PDF

2.Season Schedule: Refer to "[Template] RoboMaster University League Season Schedule" (DOWNLOAD : Click to download ( https://terra-1-g.djicdn.com/b2a076471c6c4b72b574a977334d3e05/Template%20%20RoboMaster%20University%20League%20Season%20Schedule.xlsx ) ), Excel

Submission Requirements:

1. PDF format:  Source Han Serif, 10.5pt (小四号, Chinese), or Times New Roman (English); fixed 1.5x line spacing; document naming: School + Team Name + Section Name.

2. Excel format: Font: Source Han Serif, 11pt (Chinese), or Times New Roman (English); wrap text, auto-adjust row height and column width; unless otherwise necessary, all text should be vertically centered and uniformly left-aligned or center-aligned; document naming: School + Team Name + Section Name.

* Source Han Serif font package: Click to download ( https://terra-1-g.djicdn.com/b2a076471c6c4b72b574a977334d3e05/RM2024/%E6%80%9D%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%8B%E4%BD%93.zip ).

Permissions Upon Passing:

1.Gain eligibility to the Final Robot Assessment.

2.As the season planning is closely related to the work of the team management group (including the captain, vice-captain, and project manager), the score of the season planning will serve as an important basis for selecting the annual outstanding captain (team) and annual outstanding project manager.
